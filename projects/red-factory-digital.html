A brand and website prototype for a fictional video game publisher that helps indie developers reach global audiences.

Project Overview

I started with an empathy map of a developer's worries about publishing, then built a red-and-cream identity around a factory icon. The site prototype covers services (features, design support and monetization), a client page, live chat and account registration.

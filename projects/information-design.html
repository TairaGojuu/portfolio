Information Design

Two studies in making complex information clear: a personal data visualization and the redesign of a game's back cover.

Year
2026
Course
Information Design (GRA430)
Tools
Illustrator
Type
Data visualization

The Day as a Video visualizes 14 days of my YouTube watch time (1,291 minutes) as a poster that moves from sketches to prototypes to a refined layout. The Just Dance back-cover redesign starts by mapping what confuses readers (ungrouped priorities, interleaved languages, buried data) and tests three prototypes before settling on a black-and-white hierarchy.

Academic project. Game packaging belongs to its owner and is shown for study purposes.

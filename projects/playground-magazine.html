Play//Ground Magazine

A 17-page independent magazine about action sports and culture, from snowboarding and surf to rally, wrestling and MMA.

Project Overview

I designed the cover and back cover, contents, contributors page, editor's letter and the main and short feature layouts. A bold flame masthead, condensed headlines and colour-coded features give each sport its own voice while a shared grid keeps the issue consistent.
